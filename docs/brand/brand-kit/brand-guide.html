Brand Guide · 2026

A friendly companion. Your choice of player.

Freebo connects the Emby browsing experience to the local player you choose. The guide dog gives that handoff a friendly face; the Atma wordmark gives it a name.

Primary identity

Use the supplied lockups. Keep the icon and name at the same scale relationship. On busy imagery, give the logo a clear solid surface.

Usage rules

Clear space

Keep at least 25% of the dog-head height clear around a lockup. Icon/name gap inside the lockup is 10% of the name's visible height.

Minimum sizes

Horizontal lockup: 120px on screen / 28mm in print. Dog mark: 24px. At 16px, use the supplied app icon.

Keep the identity intact

  • Use Freebo in all locales.
  • Keep the friendly expression and asymmetric ears.
  • Use the provided monochrome variants.
  • Do not stretch, recolor, add blur or separate the lockup.
  • Do not use the Chinese nickname in brand assets.
  • Free software does not mean free media.
